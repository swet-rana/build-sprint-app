Privacy policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

HealthStats is operated by Swet Rana. Contact: swet.sahay@gmail.com.

HealthStats helps you store medical reports, compare recorded results and prepare a summary for your doctor. This policy describes the current version.

Information we handle

We store uploaded reports and their filename, size, lab, patient name, report date and test type. Reports may contain sensitive health and personal information. Only upload reports you have permission to use.

Account information is used for sign-in. Google sign-in requests your basic profile and email address, not access to Gmail messages, contacts or Google Drive files. Authentication records are stored in Convex.

How information is used

We use your information to save and open reports, read supported results, compare values, prepare summaries and provide account access. Report reading and comparison run in your browser. Extracted results remain in browser memory and must be prepared again after refreshing. Reports are not sent to an external AI analysis service in this version.

Storage and providers

Convex provides hosting, database, file storage and account services. Google verifies your identity when you choose Google sign-in. These providers process information needed to operate their services.

Your browser stores an access key, report selections and sign-in information. Reports currently remain tied to the browser access key; signing in does not connect reports across devices. Clearing browser data can remove your access without deleting stored files.

Sharing

HealthStats does not automatically send your reports to doctors or other users. You choose what to print or share. The current app has no advertising or sale-of-data feature. Its providers process information to deliver the service.

Retention and deletion

Uploaded reports remain stored until permanently removed. Delete moves reports to Deleted reports, where they can be restored; it does not erase the original file. Signing out or clearing browser data does not delete server records.

Contact swet.sahay@gmail.com for access, correction or permanent-deletion requests. Permanent deletion is not available through the app. We may need information to verify your request, and browser-based report ownership can limit our ability to identify records. Do not send complete medical reports by email unless specifically needed and agreed.

Security and your choices

Report access requires your browser access key. Protect your device and avoid shared devices. No system can guarantee complete security. Keep independent copies of original reports.

Changes and questions

We will update this page when our practices change. Contact the operator above with questions about your information.
