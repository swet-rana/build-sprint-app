# Static homepage circle icons

Current homepage decoration: four static transparent PNGs inside plain 64px white circles, without the former lavender halo. Existing ribbon art and all navigation remain unchanged.

Assets: public/images/home-static-{files,document,trend,doctor}.png (256px). Generated using the built-in Image Generation tool. The comparison image uses the previous document/magnifier artwork as a reference. Final images are downscaled for efficient display.

Shared prompt: premium healthcare realistic 3D studio rendering, soft upper-left light, restrained white/lavender/deep-purple palette, transparent backgrounds, compact complete silhouettes, readable at 52px, no circle backing, halo, pedestal, text or watermark.

## Prompt subjects

### files

A compact stack of three realistic physical medical file folders, stacked horizontally with slight stagger to suggest stored records. White paper files, lavender folder tabs and subtle deep-purple spines. Visible paper thickness. Single tidy unified stack; no loose flying pages, no tray. 1024x1024 square.

### document

Use the reference's same realistic white paper and purple magnifying glass. Show ONLY ONE medical document with a small purple heading strip and faint lavender rows, NO words or numbers. One realistic magnifying glass overlays its lower right corner, polished deep-purple frame and clear glass lens. Remove the second document entirely. Do not make a sprite atlas; one compact unified icon, 1024x1024 square.

### trend

A compact rising deep-purple 3D trendline with exactly three small glossy dots: red at lower left, dark purple in the middle, green at upper right. Each dot is about 8% of icon width. The line occupies about 80% of the square canvas width and 35% of its height, with transparent margins to fit safely inside the white circle. No axes, numbers, labels or glow.

### doctor

A realistic three-dimensional miniature portrait bust of a friendly generic doctor, showing the doctor's head and shoulders, white coat with lavender collar, and a prominent dark-purple stethoscope around the neck with visible earpieces and round chestpiece. Natural medium-light skin and short dark hair, pleasant neutral expression, no exaggerated cartoon proportions. Anonymous fictional person, no likeness to any public figure. Close portrait crop but complete head, shoulders and stethoscope inside frame. Single compact medical icon, 1024x1024 square.
