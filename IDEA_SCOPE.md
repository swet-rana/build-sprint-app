IDEA LOCK · Build Sprint

Idea line: one page health history the doctor can read in 2 minutes, is what nobody offers.
(It essentially captures the trends that matter, any similar illness or diagnosis before- nobody usually handles this page to the doctor).

Who is it for: patients having reports across multiple timelines and formats

Job Done: the doctor saves substantial time in going through the reports and better understanding the root cause if applicable. Also, it takes  days for the patient to collect and share reports when the doctor asks for medical history. It solves that as well.

User: 
The trigger (when the pain hits):
I had a throat infection and visited the doctor, and she prescribed antibiotics for 7 days. No improvement and I visited the doctor again and she asked me to share the past blood reports along with any previous similar ailments. At that point, I didn't have those records, and it took me days to find all the relevant reports and share it with her. But just getting all the reports was also not enough as she then had to understand the respective health markers progression as well. Which all would have been much easier if we had all the reports under 1 banner and an AI assistant to analyse it as well.

Today's path, step by step (including not solving it at all):
1) have an ailment/issue
2) Visit the Doctor
3) Doctor asks for all relevant reports
4) You probably will have a few but not all the necessary ones if the ailment needs further diagnosis
5) You spend substantial amount of time getting all the relevant reports
6) Upon visit, the doctor sees all the reports and try to make sense out of it- again spends a lot of time
7) this usually happens because your healthcare data is spread across online and offline systems which is very difficult to make sense if not aggregated and then analysed

Goal: Time


Product:
Onboarding (how a first-time user feels the value fastest): Just upload  a couple of medical reports from your recent health check-up and see the health marker trend line of what matters most to you. It will show how measured values changed between report dates, alongside the units and reference ranges printed in each report. It will leave interpretation of those changes to the doctor. The second step onwards (when visiting a doctor) is where you also realise the value of providing the augmented and required information to the one who is  diagnosing you.
The core loop (user stories, written by me): while visiting the doctor next time, the user checks for any new reports or prescriptions to be uploaded and then runs the AI agent to check for trends and 1 page summary for the doctor
Coming back (optional): Whenever visiting the doctor 
The AI-first part (onboarding, engagement or the core loop): core loop
