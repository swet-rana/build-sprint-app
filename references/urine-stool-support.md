# Urine and stool results

Urine and stool tables are supported in native text PDFs, local OCR and server vision reading. Their results appear under the existing **Other tests** category, with explicit specimen labels. Test identity includes the specimen and printed unit; these results never enter blood/semen name matching or unit conversion.

Single finite numeric results can form trends across dates. Written findings, inequalities and cell-count ranges are retained as printed, without midpoints, numeric changes or inferred reference-range colours. Missing or uncertain results remain missing. Empty/dash units stay empty. Every accepted observation keeps its original report and physical page.

Pages combining different specimen tables that cannot be reliably separated are skipped rather than assigning all rows to one specimen. Headings and patient/specimen metadata are not results.

The model, OCR confidence threshold, spending caps, original-file cache and reading cache policy are unchanged. Existing completed cached readings are not automatically regenerated by this update. The vision instructions and response validators explicitly admit the new specimen types and written results.

Proof: `tests/specimen-results.test.mjs` and the development-only `tests/specimen-browser.html`. The browser check uses two fictional image-only PDFs, with a urine page and stool page in each report, and verifies all eight test identities, exact observations, numeric versus written presentation, Other tests placement and source links. Production refusal is checked by `tests/check-production-refusals.mjs`.
